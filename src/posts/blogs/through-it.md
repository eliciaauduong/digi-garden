title: You have to go through it
date: Last Modified
stage: seedling
---
Standing on one side of what you want feels scary. Knowing what that is doesn't really make it better. I've always taken action in anticipation of the desired outcome, predicting how my current decisions and choices would be perceived in the future, hopefully helping me get a better chance at achieving my goal. 

But when you're faced with an unknown problem, that challenge that you have to solve feels too vast, too heavy to hold. And it's hard to think many steps ahead when you don't know the destination either. 

Right now it's hard to keep going when you're in the bad skill phase. But the only way to get better is to go through it. Learn by doing.
