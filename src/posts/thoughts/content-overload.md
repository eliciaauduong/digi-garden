More content is being generated than ever - not even considering all the bot and AI slop. [[cognitive capacity|I don't think we were ever meant to digest this all]]. 

## Some questions I want to explore
- Does volume of content generation proportionately impact the scale of outcome?
- How does engagement on social media platforms translate to viewer/business value?
- How do I decide what is worth my time or attention when cost of generation is low but cost of attention is at an all-time high?
- How has my digital consumption pattern changed over time?
- What drew our obsession to metrics?
