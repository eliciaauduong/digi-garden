In a void of endless options, how do you make decisions? I've seen a lot of posts this year about taste, and how taste is the differentiating factor. From what we buy to what we watch, read or listen to, there's more content than we could ever consume. Each day, our [[cognitive capacity]] is overwhelmed, before we've even processed a single thing. So, are our choices simply the easiest option, or are they truly what we want?
