I build a world around every project - one that carries the message someone should walk away with, and holds itself together through the smallest details, where one choice quietly answers another.

I don't start with the pieces. I start with what someone should leave with, then find the connections between details that weave into a single structure.

This takes shape in two ways:

**Spaces:** things people can step into and explore.

- [[project 0]], a space to connect and create together, running monthly: digital declutter, journalling, article selection
- [[digital garden]], where thoughts and ideas are worked out in the open
- Microsites and small apps such as [[wave]]
- [[set-design]], a practice that I'm slowly exploring

**Systems:** structures built to hold the chaos so someone else doesn't have to. 

- Problem diagnosis of where complexity is breaking trust and how to resolve it
- Scalable enablement frameworks, knowledge and training systems
- Cross-functional collaboration processes
- Cohesive brand experience across digital and physical touchpoints
