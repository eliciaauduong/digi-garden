Even before I ditched Instagram and Facebook, I was already tuning my feeds to focus on content that I was interested in by managing my follows. But, I feel like that was reducing the possibility for chance discoveries that I could really enjoy. 

When I was working at Pinterest, one of the first things we learnt about was the customer journey, the path that you as a customer take from identifying something you want (awareness) to the point of purchase (conversion).

As a discovery-first platform, it made sense that although users may find ideas on Pinterest, they weren't necessarily going to buy right away. They'd switch between online and offline channels to help consider the benefits and options before making that purchasing decision. Working in marketing, the goal is to move the customers along the journey at each point to help them decide to purchase.
