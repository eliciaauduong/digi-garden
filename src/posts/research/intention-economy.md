## Questions to answer
- What is the gap between intention and action? In particular for purchases or consumption - what makes me choose one option over another in that moment?
- Intention may be an ephemeral thing. Even if I intend to buy something in the future, I may change my mind later. If that is the case, how can I continue to engage as a consumer or even as a brand?
