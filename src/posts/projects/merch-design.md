I've always been drawn to merch design - a little way to [[fandoms|support your favourite things/people]] and show your interests.

But I think merch design has more potential - beyond overpriced t-shirts and hoodies.

I want merch that whispers not screams. That signals like a secret society that others would only know if they were a part of it. A little mysterious to those who don't.

Some ideas I've been thinking about:
- Clothing designs beyond the artist's face/album + tour dates on the back, something more subtle like lyrics or an inside joke
- Behind the scenes coffee table books for your favourite album
- Professionally recorded concerts so I don't have to have my phone out or hear somebody screaming right next to the mic
