## What is project 0?
Project 0 is a space to be intentional about what you take in, and turn more of it into something new.

I believe consuming and creating work best in a loop: the right input feeds the next thing you build, and building sharpens what you go looking for next.

## What does it involve?
Each month, we'll host 3 sessions around a theme:
1. Create: learn a new skill, walk away with a finished project
2. Consume: discuss a curated list of mixed media
3. Collect: swap and share ideas or items
